# Hotel Management & Catering Specialized Module

## Overview
Traditional school ERPs fail for Hotel Management & Catering colleges because hotel management requires distinct hospitality-specific workflows:
* Commercial kitchen lab scheduling
* Food & Beverage practical ingredient requisitions
* Front Office PMS software training
* Housekeeping room management labs
* Mandatory 6-month Industrial Training (IT) in 5-star hotel properties

This module is designed directly to address the needs of the **College of Hotel Management**.

---

## 1. Key Sub-Modules

### A. Kitchen & Practical Laboratory Rostering
* **Lab Facilities:**
  * Basic Training Kitchen (BTK) - 1st Year Students
  * Quantity Food Kitchen (QFK) - 2nd Year Students
  * Advance Training Kitchen (ATK) - 3rd Year Students
  * Bakery & Confectionery Practical Lab
  * Training Restaurant & Mock Bar (F&B Service)
  * Model Front Desk & Bell Desk
  * Mock Hotel Suite / Housekeeping Lab
* **Batch Scheduling:**
  * Divide 200 students into practical kitchen batches of 20-25 students.
  * Assign Chef Instructors, menu plans, and culinary station numbers.

### B. Industrial Training (IT) / Internship Management
* Mandatory 16 to 22 weeks internship in luxury hotels (Taj, Marriott, Oberoi, Hyatt, ITC).
* **Features:**
  * **Hotel Allocation Log:** Track which student is placed at which hotel and city.
  * **Department Rotation Tracking:** 4 weeks Food Production -> 4 weeks F&B Service -> 4 weeks Housekeeping -> 4 weeks Front Office.
  * **Digital Logbook:** Students submit weekly training summaries.
  * **Appraisal Form:** Hotel HR/Mentor fills monthly rating sheet directly via a secure link.
  * **Training Certificate Storage:** Scanned upload of final hotel certificate.

### C. Culinary Equipment & Uniform Kit Inventory
* Track initial intake kits issued to new 1st-year students:
  * Chef Coat, Chef Cap, Apron & Checkered Trousers
  * Professional Chef Knife Set & Carrying Roll
  * F&B Service Waiter Cloth, Corkscrew, and Note Pad
  * Front Office Blazer & Tie
* Issue date, kit status, and replacement records.

### D. Food Festival & Event Catering Ledger
* Hotel management colleges frequently organize themed food festivals (e.g., Punjabi Dhaba, Italian Bistro, Bakery Showcase) to raise funds and train students.
* **Features:**
  * Ticket sales and guest registration.
  * Food cost budgeting and profit/loss calculation.
  * Student practical duty allocations (Kitchen Team, Service Team, Cashier Team).
