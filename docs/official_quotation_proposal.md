## 1. Executive Summary

We are pleased to submit this comprehensive commercial proposal for the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of the **Integrated Multi-Institution Trust Education ERP System**.

This platform provides a unified management ecosystem encompassing:
* **Central Trust Superadmin Web Portal:** Unified financial, admission, and administrative oversight.
* **School Management Portal & Parent Mobile App:** Admissions, fees, attendance, homework, exams, and live GPS bus tracking.
* **Hotel Management College Portal & Student Mobile App:** Academic records, fees, semester schedules, specialized practical kitchen lab rosters, 5-star hotel industrial training (internship) logs, and chef uniform/toolkit issuance.
* **Staff & Faculty Tools (Web & Mobile):** 30-second mobile attendance marker, homework publisher, and report card generator.
